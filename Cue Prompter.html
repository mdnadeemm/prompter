Cue Prompter

A teleprompter for your phone or tablet. Scrolling speeds up and slows down smoothly, follows cues you write in the script, and works with your Bluetooth remote.

Script

Cues change the scroll while you read. Put them anywhere in the script: [slow] [fast] [normal] [speed 120] [pause 2]. Slow is 25% slower, fast is 30% faster, and pause holds the text for that many seconds.

Every AI script follows the same short-video recipe. [hook] grabs attention in the first 3 seconds, [body] teaches one idea, [cta] asks for one action, and [loop] ends with half a sentence that flows straight into your first line, so the video replays smoothly. These four labels only mark sections. They don’t change the speed.

Scrolling and display

Camera and recording

With the camera on, you see yourself behind the text. The recording captures your camera and microphone, not the text. When you stop, you can watch the take and save it. If the camera is blocked, open the page in Chrome instead of inside another app.

Bluetooth remote

Pair your remote in Android’s Bluetooth settings first. Most remotes act like a keyboard. Press a button on it now to see what it sends, then assign it to an action.
